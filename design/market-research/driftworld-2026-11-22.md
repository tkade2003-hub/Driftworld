# Market Research Brief: Driftworld
**Date:** 2026-11-22   **Analyst:** game-market-research (forked)   **Data as of:** November 2026, accessed 2026-11-22
**Concept:** A free to play 3D mobile game about exploration, combat, and loot collecting on floating islands. Players glide between instanced islands on short expeditions, hunt creatures, harvest their parts, and craft gear from those parts. Revenue comes from cosmetics, a season pass at 9.99 dollars, and optional rewarded ads. No paid randomness and no purchase ever raises a combat stat.   **Genre:** Mobile action role playing game, primary. Hunt and harvest crafting plus short session action, secondary.   **Channels:** Apple App Store and Google Play, primary. Short form video and community channels for discovery.

## Recommendation
**Go with changes.** The hunt and harvest loop has proven, current, and large demand on mobile, shown by Monster Hunter Now earning an estimated 336.9 million dollars over three years and Monster Hunter Outlanders earning an estimated 10.2 million dollars in its first two weeks after launching on October 28, 2026 (both Estimated, AppMagic figures reported by PocketGamer.biz and mobilegamer.biz). The same evidence cuts the other way for a solo team: the money in this loop concentrates in Japan and in famous intellectual property, RPG spending fell about 11 percent year over year in the first half of 2025, and 62 percent of new mobile titles earn nothing at all (Sourced, Xsolla State of Play Q2 2025). The call flips to Pivot if the soft launch misses the reset retention gates set out below, or if the first ninety days of downloads track to the low case in the sizing section. The changes required are listed at the end of this section.

Changes required before Phase 2 spending:
1. Reset the soft launch retention gates. The concept document targets day one retention at or above 40 percent and day seven at or above 20 percent. GameAnalytics benchmarks for 2025 put the median game near 22 percent on day one and just under 4 percent on day seven, the top quarter just above 30 percent and 6 to 7 percent, and the top tenth near 40 percent and 11 to 12 percent (Sourced, GameAnalytics 2026 benchmarks). The concept targets describe a top decile product. Use top quarter marks as the pass gate and keep the concept numbers as stretch goals.
2. Plan Japanese localization from the start, even if it ships shortly after English. Japan supplied 63 percent of Monster Hunter Now lifetime revenue and 79 percent of the Level Infinite version revenue for Monster Hunter Outlanders in its first two weeks (Estimated, AppMagic). An English only launch closes the richest market for this exact loop.
3. Enrol in the Apple App Store Small Business Program and the Google Play Games Level Up program, and price the store fee at the reduced indie rates in every forecast (Sourced, platform fee reporting, October 2026).
4. Treat discovery as a workstream with hours attached, not a hope. The glide and the harvest are the two most clippable moments in the design, and with a marketing budget near zero they are the entire acquisition plan.
5. Keep the slice gate strict. If the slice cannot hold testers, stop and redesign the glide or the combat before building launch content, because the base rate for new titles is brutal and content volume does not fix a weak first session.

## Genre analysis
Primary genre: mobile action role playing game. Secondary genres: hunt and harvest crafting, short session action.

Lifecycle stage: mature, with the top of the market consolidating. Evidence:
- Mobile games overall took in 81.75 billion dollars of in app purchase revenue in 2025, up 1.3 percent year over year, while downloads fell 7.2 percent to 50.4 billion (Sourced, Sensor Tower State of Gaming 2026, reported January 2026). Growth now comes from keeping and monetizing existing players, not from new download volume.
- Midcore games collected 44.68 billion dollars in 2025, the most of any audience category, and role playing games made up 33.7 percent of the top 1,000 earning titles (Sourced, Sensor Tower via Game World Observer, January 2026; Appfigures 2025 Mobile Gaming Report, May 2025).
- Role playing game player spending fell about 11 percent year over year in the first half of 2025, from 10.4 billion dollars to 9.3 billion dollars, as strategy overtook it as the top genre (Estimated, AppMagic data reported by PocketGamer.biz, July 2025).
- Hybrid casual in app purchase revenue grew 20 percent to 4.2 billion dollars in 2025 (Sourced, Sensor Tower via Game World Observer, January 2026), which matters because the winning growth format borrows midcore depth and casual readability at the same time.
- Concentration is extreme: the top 1 percent of publishers took 92.5 percent of in app purchase revenue in 2025, 828 games earned more than 10 million dollars, and only nine earned more than 1 billion (Sourced, Sensor Tower via Game World Observer, January 2026).

Player expectations in this genre: a build that feels personal, gear that visibly changes play, seasons that reset goals, and a first session that delivers combat and loot inside minutes. Players also arrive with a trained suspicion of pay to win and of random draw monetization, earned from the genre leaders below.

Recent notable releases and results:
- Monster Hunter Outlanders, October 28, 2026: a full hunting and exploration role playing game from TiMi Studio Group and Capcom, published by Level Infinite and Garena. More below.
- Archero 2, January 2025: a short run action sequel from a small publisher lineage, 8 million dollars of net in app purchase revenue in its first eleven days (Estimated, AppMagic via Game World Observer, January 2025).
- Dungeon and Fighter Mobile in China remained a top three role playing game by revenue in the first half of 2025 with 286.3 million dollars on the App Store alone (Estimated, AppMagic via PocketGamer.biz, July 2025), further proof that the genre money sits in Asia and in established intellectual property.

Health signals: the genre earns at the top and starves at the median. Only 1.9 percent of new titles broke 1 million dollars of revenue in the Xsolla sample, and 62 percent earned nothing (Sourced, Xsolla State of Play Q2 2025, reported by Game Developer). SuperScale research found 83 percent of launched mobile games fail within three years, and 76 percent make their peak revenue in year one (Sourced, SuperScale Good Games Do Not Die report). Creator and press coverage of the genre is strong but follows the big franchises; a new name gets coverage for a hook, not for existing.

## Competitive landscape
### Direct

COMPETITOR
Game: Monster Hunter Outlanders   Type: direct   Channel: App Store and Google Play
Price: Free with in app purchases, including character random draws   Release: October 28, 2026
Estimated revenue: More than 10.2 million dollars of in app purchase revenue and 5.1 million downloads in just over two weeks after launch. The Level Infinite version made 7.3 million dollars from 2.2 million downloads, with Japan at 79 percent of its revenue, about 3.32 dollars per download. The Garena version made about 2.9 million dollars from 2.9 million downloads, about 1.00 dollar per download (Estimated, AppMagic via mobilegamer.biz, November 2026).
Differentiator: The Monster Hunter name, console scale hunting with squads, and Tencent distribution.
Common complaints: Early coverage flags phone heat and a large download, and the random draw system for companions draws the usual suspicion from series fans (reported in analyst coverage, November 2026).
Relevance: This is the closest product to Driftworld that has ever shipped, and it launched seven months before Driftworld soft launch. It proves the demand and occupies the shelf at the same time. Its size, squads, and random draws are the three things Driftworld can honestly contrast against.

COMPETITOR
Game: Monster Hunter Now   Type: direct   Channel: App Store and Google Play
Price: Free with in app purchases   Release: September 14, 2023
Estimated revenue: 336.9 million dollars over three years, with 219.7 million in year one, 67.3 million in year two, down 69 percent, and 49.9 million in year three, down 26 percent. Japan contributed 230.4 million dollars, 63 percent of the lifetime total (Estimated, AppMagic via PocketGamer.biz, September 2026).
Differentiator: Location based hunting in short real world sessions, built by the Niantic team now owned by Scopely.
Common complaints: The decline curve itself is the complaint made visible; players drift when the local hunting loop repeats, and spending followed them.
Relevance: The best evidence that a hunt loop tuned to short sessions earns real money on phones, and the clearest warning that the money is front loaded. Driftworld should plan its economy around a strong first year and a loyal tail, not around growth in year two.

COMPETITOR
Game: Torchlight Infinite   Type: direct, and the median case in this set   Channel: App Store, Google Play, and PC
Price: Free with in app purchases, season pass reported by a store reviewer at 15 to 20 dollars   Release: October 2022
Estimated revenue: Google Play gross in app purchase revenue estimated between 38,100 and 182,200 dollars for October 9 to November 8, 2026, a model band rather than a reported figure (Estimated, AppGazers model, November 2026). Google Play shows the 1 million plus install tier, and AppBrain counts about 2.5 million installs with a 4.4 star rating (Sourced, store listing data via AppBrain, November 2026).
Differentiator: A known personal computer action role playing name, deep build crafting, seasons, and a player trading market.
Common complaints: Store reviews praise the build depth and accuse the late game of pressure to pay; one reviewer called the maps bland and asked for more side content, quoted from the App Store listing.
Relevance: This is what a competent loot action role playing game with real intellectual property and a full studio earns four years in: a living, not a fortune. It is the sober middle of the distribution and the reason this brief refuses to size Driftworld from the hits.

COMPETITOR
Game: Diablo Immortal   Type: direct   Channel: App Store, Google Play, and PC
Price: Free with in app purchases, including paid power systems   Release: June 2, 2022
Estimated revenue: About 404 million dollars of lifetime mobile revenue and 34.4 million downloads, with 58.1 million dollars in 2025 (Estimated, AppMagic net figures via Udonis, updated July 2026). Gross consumer spending figures reported elsewhere run higher; the two methods must not be mixed.
Differentiator: The Diablo name, Blizzard and NetEase production scale, and aggressive monetization of power.
Common complaints: The monetization itself. The game remains a reference point in player discussions for paying for power, and its review scores at launch were historically low.
Relevance: Driftworld anti pillar, no purchase ever raises a combat stat, is a direct answer to the most disliked monetization in the genre. That contrast is a positioning asset, and it caps revenue per player at the same time. Both facts belong in the plan.

### Indirect

COMPETITOR
Game: Archero and Archero 2   Type: indirect   Channel: App Store and Google Play
Price: Free with in app purchases and rewarded ads   Release: 2019 and January 2025
Estimated revenue: The original passed 263 million dollars of lifetime net in app purchase revenue with nearly 96 million downloads, a lifetime return near 4 dollars per download, and the sequel made 8 million dollars in its first eleven days and more than 31 million including its soft launch period (Estimated, AppMagic via Game World Observer, January 2025; lifetime return per download from Deconstructor of Fun, 2025).
Differentiator: One thumb, one run, readable in seconds, with role playing progression wrapped around a casual core.
Common complaints: Energy limits and ad pressure in the original are the recurring grievances in coverage of the series.
Relevance: The strongest proof in this brief that a small team without a famous name can win in mobile action, and the template is readability plus short runs, not world size. Driftworld expeditions already follow that shape; the first screen and the first minute should borrow its clarity.

COMPETITOR
Game: Soul Knight   Type: indirect   Channel: App Store and Google Play
Price: Low price premium with light in app purchases   Release: 2017
Estimated revenue: Reliable current revenue figures were not found in public sources. One synthetic model estimates about 700,000 dollars per month (Unverified, Bumetric model, accessed November 2026) and should not be used for planning.
Differentiator: Pixel roguelite dungeon runs, generous free content, and years of steady updates from a small team at ChillyRoom.
Common complaints: Players praise the generosity and ask for more online play stability in community threads.
Relevance: Proof that a small team action game can live for years on goodwill and updates, at a revenue scale far below the franchise titles. A useful floor case for expectations.

### Aspirational

COMPETITOR
Game: Genshin Impact   Type: aspirational   Channel: App Store, Google Play, PC, and console
Price: Free with random draw purchases   Release: September 2020
Estimated revenue: 335 million dollars of mobile revenue in 2025, down from 465 million in 2024, and out of the annual top twenty for the first time (Estimated, AppMagic via mobilegamer.biz, 2025 grossing report). Lifetime mobile revenue near 4.8 billion dollars on the same net method.
Differentiator: Console scale open world exploration and gliding, funded at a level no indie can approach.

## Market sizing (low / middle / high)
Method: downloads in year one multiplied by net revenue per download, in app purchases only. Rewarded ad revenue is excluded because no honest public rate for this exact game exists; treat it as upside, not as a plan figure. Net revenue per download anchors: the market wide average gross revenue per download was 1.62 dollars in 2025 (Sourced, Sensor Tower data calculated by Axis Intelligence, June 2026). Archero lifetime return sits near 4 dollars per download (Estimated, Deconstructor of Fun, 2025). Monster Hunter Outlanders early return ran from 1.00 to 3.32 dollars per download depending on publisher region mix (Estimated, AppMagic, November 2026). A new name with no paid acquisition should plan below the market average.

- TAM: Mobile role playing games earned about 9.3 billion dollars in the first half of 2025 alone (Estimated, AppMagic via PocketGamer.biz, July 2025), inside total mobile game in app purchase revenue of 81.75 billion dollars for 2025 (Sourced, Sensor Tower, January 2026). The full genre is not addressable by one solo title; it is context, not a target.
- SAM: English language players of 3D action and hunting games on the two stores, reachable with no paid marketing. No public source sizes this slice directly, so it is stated as a planning bound rather than a figure: the comparable titles above drew between 2.5 million and 96 million lifetime downloads, and every one of them had either famous intellectual property, a publisher, or both.
- SOM, year one downloads (Estimated, method stated in the assumptions): low 20,000, middle 120,000, high 750,000. The high case assumes at least one store feature or one sustained piece of creator pickup; without one, treat the middle case as the ceiling.
- SOM, year one net in app purchase revenue (Estimated): low case 20,000 downloads at 0.50 dollars per download gives 10,000 dollars. Middle case 120,000 downloads at 1.30 dollars per download gives 156,000 dollars. High case 750,000 downloads at 3.00 dollars per download gives 2,250,000 dollars.
- Net revenue per download already reflects the blended store fee assumption of 20 percent and the design choice to sell style only, which lowers spend per player against genre norms that sell power or random draws.

Break even: Cash costs are small. Unity Personal is free below 200,000 dollars of revenue (Sourced, Unity pricing in the market context baseline, November 2026), store accounts are minor, and there is no office or payroll. The honest cost is founder time: 26 weeks valued at 75,000 dollars a year gives about 37,500 dollars, and valued at 150,000 dollars a year gives about 75,000 dollars (labeled assumptions, stated so the reader can substitute their own rate). At the middle revenue per download of 1.30 dollars, break even on founder time needs roughly 29,000 to 58,000 downloads. The middle case covers it. The low case does not come close. Middle case covers break even: yes, on time valued at either rate, with little margin at the higher rate.

Comparables used: Monster Hunter Now, Monster Hunter Outlanders, Diablo Immortal, Torchlight Infinite, Archero, Genshin Impact, each with figures and dates in the competitive section.

## Audience profile
Primary audience: Players who already hunt, glide, and collect in the big titles and want that loop in short sessions on a phone, plus Archero shaped players who want readable action with real progression.
Motivations: Explorers first and Achievers second, matching the concept document. Competence from reading creatures and crafting builds, autonomy from choosing islands and sets, relatedness from leaderboards and shared season goals.
Discovery channels, ranked by fit for a near zero budget: short form video of the glide and the harvest on TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels; store search and store featuring, which reward rating and retention; Reddit and Discord communities for hunting and action role playing games; creator coverage only if the slice gives them a moment worth showing. Paid acquisition is excluded by budget, and the average United States games cost per install of 1.71 dollars in 2025 (Sourced, Adjust data) would consume the entire low case revenue many times over.
Purchase drivers: a first session that crafts a real gear piece inside three minutes, visible style rewards in the pass, and trust that money never buys power. The Torchlight Infinite store reviews show this audience reads monetization closely and punishes pressure in public ratings.
Session length: the genre leaders train long sessions; Driftworld offers about five minutes per expedition and about twenty minutes per sitting, which fits commute and break play and matches the Archero pattern.
Community hubs: the Monster Hunter and action role playing subreddits and Discords are active and vocal; their recurring unmet need, visible across store reviews of the direct competitors, is a hunting game that respects time and wallet. One Torchlight Infinite reviewer asked for more side quests and map elements because the maps feel bland, which is the exact failure the concept document density rule is written to prevent.
AI sentiment: wary among vocal core players, unmeasured for the wider mobile audience. Among developers, 52 percent said generative AI is harming the industry in the GDC 2026 survey (Sourced, GDC State of the Industry, January 2026, via the market context baseline). No reliable player side survey for mobile action games was found in this run, and that gap is stated rather than filled. Practical consequence: generated assets stay logged and stay out of the marketing face of the game; the store page should sell the glide and the harvest, never the tooling.

## 2026 conditions that affect this concept
- Store fees moved in the developer favor, and the change is worth real money at indie scale. Apple still charges 30 percent standard in the United States and 15 percent under the Small Business Program. Since the April 30, 2025 court order, Apple collects no commission on United States purchases made through external links, and as of October 2026 the rate question is still in court, with Apple proposing up to 15 percent and a Supreme Court decision expected in 2027 (Sourced, platform fee reporting and Legal 500 analysis, October 2026). Google Play moved the United States, the European Economic Area, and the United Kingdom to a split fee model on June 30, 2026: a 20 percent service fee on purchases from new installs and 25 percent from existing installs, plus a 5 percent billing fee when Google Play Billing is used, with the Games Level Up program cutting one off purchase rates by 5 points from September 30, 2026 (Sourced, platform fee reporting, October 2026). Consequence for Driftworld: a web shop for the season pass becomes worth building once United States revenue is real, and every forecast must carry a fee range, not a single rate, because the Apple link rate may return.
- Downloads keep shrinking while revenue holds. Downloads fell 7.2 percent in 2025 and fell again in the first half of 2026 reporting (Sourced, Sensor Tower, January 2026 and mid 2026 index coverage). Fewer, better retained players is the market shape Driftworld is designed for, and it raises the cost of a weak first session.
- The direct competitor launched into Driftworld exact window. Monster Hunter Outlanders arrived October 28, 2026 with publisher marketing, a China launch still to come in 2027, and a quarterly content cadence (Estimated figures and reported plans, AppMagic via mobilegamer.biz, November 2026). Driftworld soft launch near late May 2027 lands inside that cycle. The response is contrast, not silence: short expeditions, no random draws, no paid power, and a download a fraction of its size.
- Paid randomness is regulated harder every year, and Driftworld already complies by design. The concept sells no random items at all, which keeps it clear of the stricter age ratings and disclosure duties that attach to paid random items in the June 2026 PEGI criteria recorded in the concept document, and clear of store odds disclosure rules entirely.
- AI built supply floods the bottom of both stores. The market context baseline records itch.io deleting bulk AI uploads on November 3, 2026 and Steam adding pre review checks on November 4, 2026 (Sourced, baseline references, November 2026). Mobile stores have the same flood with less public reporting. Consequence: craft signals matter. Hand tuned feel, a coherent art direction from the modular kit, and honest store media are how Driftworld reads as made, not generated.
- Experienced developers are going solo after 10,977 industry layoffs in 2026 by October 20 (Sourced, baseline references, October 2026). The quality bar for a solo launch in a visible genre is higher than it was three years ago, which strengthens the case for the strict slice gate.

## Positioning
**Statement:** For mobile players who love the hunt and harvest loop but have five minutes rather than fifty, Driftworld is a 3D action exploration game where every gear piece is a part you harvested yourself on a floating island you glided to. Unlike Monster Hunter Outlanders, it sells no random draws and no power, only style, and every expedition ends inside one short sitting.
**Differentiator:** The harvest is the loot, and the wallet never touches strength. One screenshot shows it: a player gliding between islands. One clip shows it better: the harvest moment, parts rising off a fallen elder into a gear piece. The differentiator is defensible on honesty and session shape, not on technology; a large studio could copy the structure, which is why speed to a loyal niche matters more than secrecy.
**Price:** Free to play, season pass at 9.99 dollars per season, a 4.99 dollar one time starter pack, cosmetics at direct stated prices. For comparison, a Torchlight Infinite store reviewer reported its season pass at 15 to 20 dollars and still called it worth it, so 9.99 dollars sits comfortably under the genre reference point (Sourced, App Store review text, accessed November 2026).
**Window:** Soft launch near late May 2027 per the concept timeline, one region first. Before fixing the global date, check the Outlanders season calendar and the HoYoverse update calendar and keep two clear weeks from both; those dates were not verifiable in this run and must be checked again in Phase 4.
